## Question

Depends on [Assess Godot for the Android composer](02-godot-android-feasibility.md) and [Test the composition workflow on the phone](03-phone-touch-prototype.md).

After the touch trial and feasibility research, should the Android composer use Godot, continue with web technology, or require another focused experiment before choosing?

Compare the accepted mobile interaction, audio needs, local project recovery and exchange, testing, implementation effort, and device evidence. Distinguish reusing the musical specification from reusing the current JavaScript implementation. Do not treat a successful browser trial as proof of native Godot behavior, or documentation as a latency measurement. If missing evidence prevents a sound choice, create the smallest additional experiment rather than forcing a decision. Record an ADR only when a real trade-off has been accepted and the choice warrants one.
