## Question

Depends on [Define the phone writing workflow](01-touch-workflow.md).

Can the owner comfortably write and correct a short phrase on the Redmi Note 9 using the agreed touch controls while retaining the independent parts, chord boxes, harmony displays, and piano preview?

Adapt the current browser prototype after the gesture and workspace agreement. Preserve a usable desktop reference. Test the accepted note-entry and correction task, sustained notes, selection, movement/resizing, navigation, and playback. Confirm a working phone-accessible delivery path before requesting a trial. Surface state and keep the artifact disposable; use the existing TDD seams for musical behavior and actual phone interaction for the usability verdict. Record what was observed, what remains untested, and the owner's reaction.
