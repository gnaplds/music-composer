Help & tutorials

Your compositions save in this browser. Download a project file to keep a separate copy or move it to another device.

Start here: write your first notes
  1. Close this panel with the × button.
  2. Choose Melody, Chord, or Bass in Part.
  3. Choose a Note duration, such as Quarter or Half.
  4. Tap a piano key to add a note.
  5. Tap Play to hear your music. Tap it again to pause.

Your edits save in this browser. Look at the save status beside the composition name.

On a computer, click instead of tap. The amber entry cursor marks the position for new notes.

Melody moves the cursor after each note. Chord and Bass keep it still for notes that play together.

Next step is beside the piano. Tools contains Advance after entry, which moves the cursor after each note.

Change notes on a phone
  1. Tap Select.
  2. Choose Notes.
  3. Tap each note that you want to change.
  4. Tap Move or Resize.
  5. Drag a selected note.
  6. Release your finger to apply the change.

A chord box tap selects all notes that sound inside it. More box taps add notes to the selection.

Held notes count too. Move, Resize, and Delete affect each whole note. Clear removes only the selection.

Move changes the note position. Resize changes the note duration. Outside Move or Resize, a swipe scrolls the timeline.

  • To remove selected notes, tap Delete.
  • To reverse the last edit, tap Undo.
  • To stop an unfinished edit, tap Cancel.
Change notes on a computer
  • Drag a note to change its position.
  • Drag the right edge to change its duration.
  • Click a note to open its pitch and duration settings.
  • To select several notes, hold Ctrl or Cmd as you click them.
  • Right-click a note to remove it.
  • Click Undo to reverse the last edit.
  • After you select a chord box, press Space to play or pause.
Find a position or adjust the view
  • Drag the amber entry handle to choose a note position.
  • On a phone, swipe the timeline to see other notes.
  • On a computer, hold the right mouse button as you drag the timeline.
  • Drag the handle above the piano to change its height.
  • Tap an Octave button to see higher or lower piano notes.
  • On a phone, tap Hide piano controls to make more space.

Show piano controls restores the controls. Hide parts hides all three note rows. Their notes still play.

Change the sound
  • Move a Vol slider to change the volume of that part.
  • On a phone, open Tools for the playback settings.

BPM controls the speed. Metronome adds a beat sound. Loop measures repeats the chosen measures.

Save or move your work

Tap the composition name to create, open, rename, copy, or move work to Trash. Restore a project from Trash, or delete it forever after confirmation.

Download project saves a separate file. Import project opens a new copy. Export MIDI shares the notes with music software.

Projects save only in this browser. Download a project file before you move to another device. When the app says Ready offline, you can reopen it without internet.

Understand the chord boxes

Each box shows a chord name and a Roman numeral. The Roman numeral describes the chord in the current key.

Bass and Chord notes determine the name. Melody notes do not change it.

The collection defines the piano note groups. Empty and new boxes use its key during Next step or piano entry.

Boxes with notes keep their key. A key change does not move notes.

Limits and note list

Compositions save in this browser. Project files include the full editable composition. MIDI export shares the notes with music software; it does not include chord boxes or custom labels. MIDI import and audio export are not available. The app uses a temporary synth sound and supports up to 256 quarter notes.

Some chords have no automatic name. You can still play them.
